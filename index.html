# SierraBuy Privacy Policy **Last updated:** March 24, 2026 SierraBuy we operates the SierraBuy mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. --- ## 1. Information we collect ### a. Account and contact information When you create or use an account, we may collect: - **Email address** and **phone number** (if you choose to provide them) - **Authentication credentials** (for example, through your sign-in provider) - **Profile information** you add (such as display name, bio, photo, and preferences) ### b. Sign-in providers We may use **Sign in with Apple** and **Google Sign-In**. Those providers may share with us information such as your name, email address, and Apple/Google user identifiers, according to your choices and their policies. ### c. Content you provide We collect content you submit in the App, including **text, images, listings, messages**, and other materials you upload or send (for example, marketplace listings, WeLinko profile content, or chat messages). ### d. Location information With your permission, we may collect **device location**, including **precise GPS coordinates** when you allow location access, to show **nearby listings**, improve **local marketplace relevance**, and **reverse geocode** your position into a **city or region** (for example, to label or filter content). Depending on your device settings and the permissions you grant, the App may also request **location when the app is in the background** (as described in the system permission prompts). We use location information only as described here and in the in-app permission messages. If you do not grant location access, certain features may be limited. ### e. Identifiers and device data We may collect **user IDs**, **device identifiers**, **push notification tokens** (for Firebase Cloud Messaging), and **technical data** such as app version, OS version, and diagnostic information needed to operate and secure the App. ### f. Usage and analytics We use **Firebase Analytics** (and related Firebase services) to understand how the App is used, such as **screen views**, **feature usage**, and **events** (for example, actions you take in the App). This helps us improve performance and reliability. ### g. Payments - **In-app purchases and subscriptions** (for example, WeLinko+, Promote Plus, boosts) are processed by **Apple** through **Apple In‑App Purchase**. - Certain **payments** (for example, marketplace or vendor checkout flows) may be processed by **Stripe** or other payment processors we integrate. We do **not** receive or store your full payment card number or bank account credentials on our servers. Payment processing is subject to **Apple’s** and **Stripe’s** (or the applicable processor’s) privacy policies and terms. --- ## 2. Information we do not collect (directly) We do **not** use the App to collect **credit scores**, **income**, or **asset** information for lending purposes. We do **not** intend to collect payment card or bank **primary account numbers** ourselves; those are handled by **Apple** and **Stripe** (or other payment processors) as part of checkout. --- ## 3. How we use information We use the information above to: - Operate, maintain, and improve the App - Provide marketplace, social, messaging, and related features - Personalize content (including **location-based** relevance where permitted) - Authenticate users and prevent fraud or abuse - Send **service-related** messages and **push notifications** (where you consent) - Analyze usage to improve features and performance - Comply with law and respond to lawful requests --- ## 4. How we share information We may share information with: - **Service providers** that help us host data, deliver the App, process payments, or send notifications (including **Google Firebase**, **Google Maps**, **Google Sign-In**, **Stripe**, and **Apple** for in-app purchases). - **Other users**, when you choose to post public listings, profile content, or send messages—according to your settings and the features you use. We do not sell your personal information as defined under applicable U.S. state “sale” laws (where those laws apply). We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you. --- ## 5. Data retention and security We retain information as long as needed to provide the App and for legitimate business purposes (such as security, legal compliance, and dispute resolution). We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. --- ## 6.
